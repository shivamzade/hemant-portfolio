Luxury EV Body and Closures

Advanced body and closure systems engineering for premium electric vehicles, focusing on innovative design solutions that meet the unique requirements of luxury EV platforms.

  • Company: Faraday Future
  • Role: Principal Design Release Engineer, Body & Closures
  • Category: Premium / Electric Vehicle
  • Key Focus: Body Structure & Closure Systems

This project involved leading the development of body structure and closure systems for the FF91 luxury electric vehicle, representing the cutting edge of premium EV design and engineering. The work required innovative approaches to meet the unique challenges of electric vehicle architecture while maintaining the highest standards of luxury vehicle quality and performance.

As Principal Design Release Engineer, I directed cross-functional teams across USA and China for both FF91 and FF81 vehicle programs, overseeing body structure, closures engineering, tooling development, and launch readiness. The project involved extensive work with aluminum structures (5xxx/6xxx series) for components including hoods, fenders, liftgate CIW, and front-end structures.

Key technical challenges included balancing pedestrian protection requirements (ECE R127) with structural durability for innovative hood concepts, managing offsite liftgate design execution ensuring FMVSS 305 and 206 compliance, and resolving complex engineering issues such as fender dog leg flexing for improved fit and finish. The project demonstrated advanced capabilities in managing global teams, coordinating complex supply chains, and delivering premium-quality closure systems for next-generation electric vehicles.
