Classic Mustang (1965-1968) Door Implementation

Engineering management for the reproduction of classic 1965-1968 Ford Mustang and Shelby GT models through advanced reverse engineering and modern manufacturing techniques.

See you tube video from 2:33 to 2:36 seconds into the video.Click here

  • Company: Revology Cars
  • Role: Engineering Manager, Body Closures
  • Category: Specialty / Premium Heritage
  • Key Focus: Door Systems & Body Engineering

This project involved leading the Body Engineering team responsible for the design, validation, and release of mechanical and structural components for the faithful reproduction of 1965–1970 Ford Mustang and Shelby GT models. The work required bridging historical vehicle architecture with modern manufacturing principles through advanced reverse engineering methods.

As Engineering Manager, I successfully introduced contemporary engineering practices into a legacy rework environment, ensuring that classic designs met modern quality, reliability, and durability standards while maintaining authentic heritage characteristics. The project involved directing full development cycles from concept through production, collaborating closely with suppliers to ensure high-quality tooling and manufacturability.

Key achievements included improving overall quality and reliability while maintaining the iconic design elements that make these vehicles timeless classics. The team focused on enhancing customer satisfaction through better fit and finish, improved functionality, and enhanced durability, all while preserving the authentic look and feel that Mustang enthusiasts expect. This project demonstrates how modern engineering can enhance classic designs without compromising their historical significance.
