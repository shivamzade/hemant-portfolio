Passenger SUV and Truck - Frontend Sheet Metal, Closures

Advanced engineering development for front-end sheet metal and closure systems on passenger SUV and truck platforms, focusing on structural integrity and manufacturing efficiency.

  • Company: General Motors
  • Role: Senior Design Release Engineer
  • Category: Mass Market
  • Key Focus: Frontend Structures & Sheet Metal

This project involved comprehensive front-end sheet metal design and development for GM's Advanced Vehicle Development (AVD) program, focusing on both passenger SUV and truck platforms. The work required innovative approaches to frontend structure design while maintaining GM's high standards for quality, safety, and manufacturability.

As Senior Design Release Engineer, I was responsible for the design and development of front and rear closure systems, along with critical front-end sheet metal components. A key achievement was the development of the front lower corner BIW structure, including tubes and stampings specifically designed to support truck headlamp systems while optimizing for crash performance and assembly efficiency.

The project demonstrated expertise in balancing multiple engineering requirements including structural integrity, aerodynamic performance, pedestrian safety, and manufacturing feasibility. The work involved extensive collaboration with cross-functional teams including styling, aerodynamics, safety engineering, and manufacturing to ensure optimal integration of all frontend components. This project showcases advanced capabilities in body engineering for high-volume mass-market vehicles with complex requirements.
